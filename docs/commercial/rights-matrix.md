# B-01 commercial rights matrix

This is the central licensing record for Phase 5. It was last verified on **2026-11-05** at baseline `a2f89c71`. Bracketed IDs point to [evidence-ledger.md](evidence-ledger.md).

**Readiness labels:**
- `CLEARED`: documented, affirmative authorisation for the specific commercial use. **No row is CLEARED.**
- `CONDITIONAL`: may proceed only after named conditions are met.
- `UNRESOLVED`: insufficient evidence or unsettled interpretation. This is the default.
- `PROHIBITED`: a directly applicable, verified restriction rules the use out, in the absence of a licence.

**Evidence, risk and clearance (2026-11-09 reconciliation)**

**The B-01 audit identified commercial, licensing and legal risks. It did not establish a verified contractual breach, regulatory violation, or judicial finding of unlawful conduct by F1 Stories.**

| Layer | What the documents may establish |
|---|---|
| Observable implementation facts | Source or dated live checks show what is displayed, fetched, stored or published; they do not prove payments or permissions. |
| Published rights-holder terms | P01–P07 record stated restrictions and licence conditions; publication alone does not establish their application or enforceability against F1 Stories. |
| Interpretation of those terms | Whether a particular placement or dataset falls within a term is an interpretation, marked INFERENCE or REQUIRES_LEGAL_REVIEW. |
| Possible current exposure | Existing placements and dependencies may create current risk; this is not a verified breach or violation. |
| Required professional assessment | Qualified counsel assesses applicable law and terms; a tax adviser assesses tax and registration. Their advice is not a rights-holder permission. |
| Affirmative clearance or provider permission | Record the actual use, assets, scope, provider or rights-holder permission where required, remaining conditions and owner approval. Neither CI nor a narrower blocker supplies this evidence. |

`PROHIBITED (rights-holder policy)` records the published-policy gate. It does not determine infringement or exclude a professional assessment of applicable law. Regulatory gates must be resolved under the relevant regulatory process. No scenario becomes cleared because a blocker was narrowed.

Two further points apply to every row:
- **Readiness is about new commercial use only.** It is not a finding that current editorial publication is unlawful, and it is not an instruction to change the live site. See [b01-closeout.md](b01-closeout.md) Q4.
- **"Owner" means the person who decides the row.** "Site owner" decides business questions. "Counsel" means a qualified lawyer or tax adviser, who must advise before the site owner decides.

## 4. Scenario comparison

X1–X4 require scenario-specific assessment; they are not four automatic blockers for every format:

- **X1** F1 brand and domain rights: assess the actual use of the current identity, naming and sponsor association [CR-01]. Domain control is a separate factual question [CR-06].
- **X2** Existing-partner relationships and logo permissions: assess P1–P6 marks and relationships actually included or relevant to a proposed placement [CR-02]. Missing records for an unrelated partner do not automatically block a different sponsorship; the new sponsor's own logo and contract permissions remain required.
- **X3** Publisher/provider information and commercial disclosure: assess general information requirements [CR-07, P15, P17, U02] separately from disclosures for the specific paid format [P12–P15, P18, U01]. The draft policy is a proposal, not proof of compliance or a universal statutory prerequisite.
- **X4** Commercial and editorial approval: separate approvals and human publication safeguards apply to paid editorial projects [CR-08]. Paid-article auto-merge is not automatically a blocker for a static logo/link alone.

This is a **governance applicability** matrix, not a legal determination or an overall readiness classification. `APPLICABLE` means the described proposal has a relevant decision/permission gate. `CONDITIONAL` states a trigger that must be checked; where the current strip or page already meets it, its gate is active. `NOT DIRECTLY RELEVANT` means the named blocker does not directly govern that narrow format; it does not waive business approval, rights or other safeguards.

X1 assesses operating under the present identity for each actual format; an `APPLICABLE` cell does not predetermine whether a new F1 licence, a scoped permission, a different implementation or another professional recommendation will be needed. X2 concerns existing P1–P6 relationships/logos; permission for a **new** sponsor's own mark remains an additional gate even when X2 is conditional. X3 has two distinct questions within the existing ID: general publisher/provider information, and disclosures for the selected commercial format. X4 means editorial approval and paid-content auto-merge safeguards, not ordinary approval of a static commercial agreement.
