## Shared contract and prerequisites

These rules form part of both initial definitions. All outputs and terms below are proposed and uncosted. Unset commercial values, dates, categories and named operational owners are **TBD — OWNER DECISION**. The publisher controls editorial judgment and publication; a sponsor has no influence over independent conclusions. Neither concept implies Formula 1 endorsement or official association. No unlicensed image, racing data or third-party material may be redistributed. No traffic, conversions, engagement or other performance is guaranteed. No newsletter, video, tool placement, BetCast placement, exclusivity or renewal entitlement is included. No new series hub, paid ranking or social post is promised.

Before an offer or delivery, resolve the applicable identity, actual distribution, publisher information, disclosure, provider, media and privacy gates; document permission for the new sponsor's name, logo and placement and for actual included existing marks and obligations. Complete owner category and business decisions, professional tax and legal review as applicable, and a private scope and contract (CR-01–CR-10, BR-12–BR-14). Current placements have separate risks and are not approved by this proposal. See [rights matrix](rights-matrix.md) and [risk register](risk-register.md); pricing inputs and methodology are in [pricing methodology](pricing-methodology.md).

Manual records can support fulfillment without new analytics or a database. Approved scope and dated evidence are needed: campaign dates, publications and commitments are not currently reconciled campaign measures. Source code can collect consented main-site outbound clicks; a sponsored aggregate model was recorded as executed, but partner attribution is unvalidated and no sponsor report is ready. Consent gaps, blocked scripts, modified clicks and lost beacons limit coverage; clicks are intent, not conversions. Sponsor impressions, all-visitor reach, external conversions, incremental revenue and cross-app unique users are not measured. B-03 is evidence validation under the brief. B-06 responsibilities are proposed and owner-defined; any implementation requires separate authorisation. See the [measurement states and limits](pricing-methodology.md#performance-evidence-and-handoff) (R14–R16, CR-10, BR-11).
