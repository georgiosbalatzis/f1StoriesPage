Reading this comparison

Preview images show the same viewport; click any image for the complete page. “Mobile preview” and “Desktop preview” open a working page inside a frame of the specified width. Links for supporting stories, author profiles and additional reports open the existing F1Stories website. The main story, homepage and driver/constructor reports stay in the selected concept.

The standings are the repository’s dated snapshot: 2026, round 11, generated 22 August 2026. BetCast #270 is dated 25 July 2026. Team Radio is an invitation to contact the team, with no invented live state.

Fixed art direction: A uses a paper edition; B combines a dark media shell with a light reading surface; C uses cool light paper. These prototypes do not offer a global theme switch.
